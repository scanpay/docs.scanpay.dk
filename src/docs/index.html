Getting started

We have developed a payment platform that allows companies to accept payments online. The platform is programmed in C and designed with a secure-by-design approach. The emphasis has been on developing a secure, stable and efficient platform with a small and auditable codebase.

We have put much thought into our API and hope you enjoy working with it. If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to email us or chat with us on our IRC server irc.scanpay.dev:6697.

Environments

We have two distinct environments: a production and a sandbox environment. While they are not connected in terms of credentials or data, they are functionally identical.

  • Production Environment is our live, PCI-certified environment, which processes thousands of payments daily. This environment is specifically designed and configured for production and production testing.
  • Sandbox Environment is a versatile testing ground that developers can use to test their applications and integrations. You can read more about the sandbox and how to gain access here.
