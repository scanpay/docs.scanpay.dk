Transactions

In our terminology, a transaction is a payment authorized by the cardholder's bank. You can create a transaction through the payment link or subscriptions API. Once authorized, transactions can be captured, refunded, or voided, meaning:

  • Authorized: The money has been reserved on the cardholder's account.
  • Capture: A transfer of some or all of the authorized amount from the cardholder's account to the merchant's account.
  • Refund: A transfer of some or all of the captured amount from the merchant's account back to the cardholder's account.
  • Void: The reservation made by authorizing has been removed and the amount can no longer be captured.

Transactions can be partially authorized, captured, or refunded, but not partially voided.
