A list of the services used when authorizing the transaction.

Currently
<code class="hljs-string">"3dsecurev2"</code>,
<code class="hljs-string">"applepay"</code>, and
<code class="hljs-string">"mobilepay"</code>
are supported.
Additionally,
<code class="hljs-string">"3dsecure"</code> and
<code class="hljs-string">"dsbn"</code>
can appear on older transactions.

The acquiring agreement used is also present in the form
<code class="hljs-string">"clearhaus-2"</code>,
<code class="hljs-string">"nets-1"</code>,
and so on, matching what you see in the dashboard.
