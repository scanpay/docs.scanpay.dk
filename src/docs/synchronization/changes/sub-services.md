A list of the services used when first
[creating](/subscriptions/create-subscriber/) or last
[renewing](/subscriptions/renew-subscriber/) the subscriber.

Currently
<code class="hljs-string">"3dsecurev2"</code> and
<code class="hljs-string">"applepay"</code>
are supported.
Additionally,
<code class="hljs-string">"3dsecure"</code> and
<code class="hljs-string">"dsbn"</code>
can appear on older transactions.

The acquiring agreement used is also present in the form
<code class="hljs-string">"shift4-1"</code>,
<code class="hljs-string">"nets-2"</code>,
and so on, matching what you see in the dashboard.
